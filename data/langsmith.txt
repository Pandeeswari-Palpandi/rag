LangSmith is a developer platform designed for building, testing, debugging, monitoring, 
and evaluating LLM and AI agent applications. It works especially well with frameworks such as LangChain 
and helps developers trace every step of an AI application, including prompts, model calls, tool usage, 
outputs, errors, and execution time. LangSmith also provides features for prompt testing, 
dataset creation, evaluation, observability, and performance monitoring, making it easier to identify 
problems and improve the quality and reliability of AI applications. In simple terms, LangChain helps 
you build AI applications, while LangSmith helps you understand, test, and improve how those 
applications work.
